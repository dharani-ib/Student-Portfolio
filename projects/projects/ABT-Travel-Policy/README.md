# ABT Travel Policy Benchmarking

## Project Overview
A comparative study of ABT's employee travel policy with six other companies to understand differences in travel benefits and policies.

## Objective
To identify gaps in ABT's travel policy and explore opportunities to improve employee benefits and travel-related support.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Comparative Research

## My Work
- Compared ABT's travel policy with six other companies.
- Studied travel allowances, accommodation, transportation, and employee benefits.
- Identified policy differences and missing benefits.
- Presented findings through a dashboard.

## Key Findings
Some competitor policies offered benefits such as night travel facilities, local conveyance, mobile allowances, and internet allowances that were not included in the policy being studied.

## Outcome
Identified opportunities for policy improvement through comparative analysis and research.
