# Marico vs Dabur – Financial Comparison

## Project Overview
A comparative study of Marico Limited and Dabur India Limited to understand their financial performance and business position.

## Objective
To compare two FMCG companies and understand how financial information can be used to evaluate and compare businesses.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Financial Statement Analysis

## My Work
- Studied the financial information of both companies.
- Compared selected financial indicators.
- Examined similarities and differences in their performance.
- Organized the comparison to understand each company's financial position.

## Outcome
Developed practical understanding of company comparison, financial analysis, and the use of financial information in business decision-making.
